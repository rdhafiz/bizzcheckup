Canonical and robots: the one true address of the page, and whether search engines may list it (reports and other private pages: noindex). 2. Browser: theme colour (the phone's address bar) and the home-screen icon. 5. Structured data (JSON-LD): facts search engines and AI assistants read. {% endcomment %}{% load static %} {# 1.

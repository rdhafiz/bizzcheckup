In short

  • We only look at public pages of the website you enter.
  • Your name and email are optional, and never sold or shared.
  • No trackers and no advertising cookies.

Who is responsible

{{ branding.legal.operator }} runs {{ product_name }} and decides how your data is used. You can reach them at {{ branding.email }}.

What we collect

  • The website address you enter, and what we find on its public pages (the same pages any visitor can see).
  • Your name and email, only if you give them. They're optional.
  • A scrambled fingerprint of your IP address (a salted hash). We use it only to stop the same visitor starting too many check-ups. Your actual IP address is never stored.

Why

  • To run the check-up and show you the report and PDF.
  • To keep the service fair: limits per visitor, and protection against bots.
  • If you left your email and agreed: so {{ branding.first_name }} can contact you about your report. No newsletters, and we never sell or share your details.

Who can see your report

Each report has a long, random link that can't be guessed. Anyone you share that link with can open the report, and nobody else can find it. Reports are hidden from search engines. If someone checks the same website within {{ reuse_hours }} hours, they're shown the same report.

Cookies

We set only one cookie: a security cookie that protects the form (CSRF). If you switch between light and dark mode, your browser remembers that choice locally. There are no trackers and no advertising cookies. The details are in the cookie policy.

How long we keep it

Reports and contact details are kept until you ask us to delete them, or until we no longer need them to run the service.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you. To have a report or your contact details deleted, email {{ branding.email }} with the report link.

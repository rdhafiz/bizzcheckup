In short

  • Check your own websites, or ones you have permission to check.
  • Don't use {{ product_name }} to attack, overload or copy anyone's website.
  • Website owners can ask us to stop checking their site.

You may

  • Check websites you own, run or manage.
  • Check a website with its owner's permission, for example as their agency or developer.
  • Share your reports with your team, clients or anyone who can help.

You may not

  • Use {{ product_name }} to attack, test the security of, or overload a website without its owner's permission.
  • Get around our limits ({{ rate_limit }} check-ups an hour per visitor), our bot protection, or the blocking of private and internal network addresses.
  • Start check-ups with scripts or automated tools.
  • Copy, resell or rebrand the service or its reports as your own product.
  • Present a report as an official certificate, audit or legal proof of compliance.

How we visit websites

{{ product_name }} reads up to {{ max_pages }} public pages, like a visitor would. It never logs in, fills in forms or reads private areas. When it looks for more pages to check, it follows the website's robots.txt rules. Our visits identify themselves with BizzCheckup in the browser name (User-Agent), so site owners can see who visited.

If you own a website

If you don't want your website checked, email {{ branding.email }} with its address and we'll stop checking it.

If this policy is broken

We may stop a check-up, block access or delete reports, without notice. Serious abuse may be reported to the people affected or to the authorities.

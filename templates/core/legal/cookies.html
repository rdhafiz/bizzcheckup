What a cookie is

A cookie is a small piece of text a website asks your browser to keep and send back on the next visit. "Local storage" is similar, but it never leaves your browser.

Cookies we set

NameWhyHow long
csrftokenSecurity: proves the check-up form was sent from this website, not by someone else (protection against "cross-site request forgery").1 year

In short

  • Scores are automated estimates and can change from one check to the next.
  • A report is not a security audit or a legal certificate.
  • For important decisions, ask a professional.

Automated estimates

Reports are made by software, in about a minute, from up to {{ max_pages }} public pages. Scores can differ between checks because of network speed, the time of day, caching, or a firewall that treats automated visitors differently. Speed results come from Google PageSpeed Insights.

What a report is not

  • Not a security audit. The security checks look at what any visitor can see. They are not a penetration test, and a good score doesn't mean a site is safe.
  • Not proof of legal compliance. The accessibility checks find common problems, but automated tools can't confirm that a site meets WCAG or any accessibility law.
  • Not a promise of rankings or sales. Following the advice can improve visibility, but no one can guarantee Google rankings, AI recommendations or customers.

Advice, not instructions

The fixes in a report are general advice. Before changing your website, make a backup and, for anything important, ask a qualified developer. We are not responsible for changes made based on a report.
